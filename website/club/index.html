How to join

  1. Install Chomp and sign in with Apple (Profile → Settings → Online play).
  2. Tap Open in Chomp, or go to Profile → Clubs & friends and enter the code.
  3. Run. Every coin you collect now counts for the club.

Joining a club is free. You can be in one club at a time.
