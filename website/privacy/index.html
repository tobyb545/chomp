Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

The short version
  • Chomp needs your location to turn runs into captured streets. No one else ever sees your live location or your routes.
  • Privacy zones trim the start and end of runs on your phone, before anything is uploaded.
  • Other players see your runner name, your colour and which streets you hold. That's it.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling data. No third-party analytics.
  • You can play fully offline or privately, and delete your online account from the app at any time.

Who we are

Chomp ("we", "us") is a running game for iPhone made by an independent developer in the United Kingdom. We're the data controller for the personal data described here. You can reach us through the contact page.

What stays on your phone

Chomp works without an account. Played offline or in private mode, everything stays on your iPhone:

  • Your full GPS recordings of runs, your run history and stats.
  • Anything read from Apple Health (workouts, workout routes and heart rate), which is used to add runs you forgot to record and to check runs are genuine.
  • Your privacy zones, settings, runner name and colour.

Health data is never used for advertising or sold, and is only uploaded as part of a run you submit (see below).

What other players can see

  • Your runner name and colour.
  • Which streets and coins you hold, and roughly when (to the hour) you last captured them.
  • Your coin totals on leaderboards, and your club.

They can't see your routes, your run times, your live location or your privacy zones. In private mode you don't appear at all.

Location

Chomp asks for location permission when you start using it. It uses your location to show the streets around you and, during a run, to record the run, including with the screen off. iPhone always shows when location is in use. Chomp doesn't record your location when you're not running, and never in the background outside a run.

Map data comes from OpenStreetMap services. To load the streets around you, your phone requests map tiles for your area from those services, which see your IP address and the area requested, as any website would.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and servers.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications, Apple Health on your device, and payments.
  • RevenueCat: manages Chomp Pro subscriptions.
  • OpenStreetMap services (Overpass and OpenFreeMap): street and map data.
  • Strava: only if you choose to connect it.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use legal safeguards such as the UK's International Data Transfer Addendum or adequacy decisions.

Our legal bases

  • Providing the game you asked for (contract): your account, runs, streets and leaderboards.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the game fair (anti-cheat), safe (reports and blocks) and working.
  • Consent: location, Apple Health and notifications, which you grant in iOS and can withdraw in Settings at any time. Health data is special category data; we process it only with your explicit consent.

How long we keep it

We keep your online data while you have an account. Delete it in the app (Profile → Settings → Delete online account) and your profile, runs, run traces, streets and club memberships are removed from our servers straight away. Reports you made about others may be kept as long as needed to deal with them. Data on your phone stays until you delete the app.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, and object to or restrict how we use it. Contact us through the contact page. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Chomp isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address. Invite and club pages read the code from the link in your browser only; it isn't sent to us.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
