Report a player

Cheating or an offensive name? Tap their name on a leaderboard in the app and choose Report. Reports are private and reviewed by a person. You can also mute or block them there.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete online account. This removes your profile, runs and coins from our servers straight away.

Subscriptions

Chomp Pro is billed by Apple. Manage or cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.
