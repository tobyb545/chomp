## Tips
- The reels have no sound. Add a trending track in Instagram when you post: reels with audio get much more reach.
- Post the reels first. They reach people who don't follow you yet; the carousel works better once people land on your profile.
- Pin the carousel to your profile grid so new visitors see what the app is in one swipe.
- Reply to comments with "Which town are you in?" to spark rivalry (and engagement).
